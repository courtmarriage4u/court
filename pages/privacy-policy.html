Court Marriage For U (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy and confidentiality of visitors who access our website (courtmarriage4u.com). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard personal information shared with us through this website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out our consultation or registration forms, send us an email, or contact us via phone/WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Full Name
  • Phone / WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • Current City and Country of Residence
  • General nature of your legal inquiry (please avoid submitting confidential case documents through the general website form)
  • Basic technical data (such as browser type, IP address, and pages visited) collected automatically via standard website analytics or cookies to ensure smooth website performance.

2. Purpose of Collecting Information

We process your personal data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule requested legal consultations.
  • To communicate appointment confirmations, updates, or administrative information.
  • To improve the functionality, security, and user experience of our website.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

  • Strict Privacy: We do not sell, rent, trade, or commercially distribute your personal information to any third parties.
  • Limited Access: Information submitted through our website is accessed only by authorized members of our legal and administrative team.
  • Note on Privilege: While we treat all website inquiries with strict confidentiality, formal advocate-client privilege under Indian law attaches only after our firm conducts a conflict check and formally accepts your engagement.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures (such as SSL encryption and restricted access controls) to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain your contact details only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected or as required by applicable law. Form submissions are kept for one year and then deleted, unless needed for an ongoing engagement or required by law.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have submitted to us through this website. To exercise these rights, please write to us at courtmarriage4u@gmail.com.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external statutory portals or informational resources (such as court websites or legal aid authorities). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

7. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
