Documents

Documents usually required

A general list only. We confirm the exact requirements for your type of marriage.

  • Proof of age for both parties, such as a 10th marksheet or birth certificate
  • Proof of address for both parties, such as Aadhaar or another government ID
  • Recent passport-size photographs of both parties
  • Identity proof of the witnesses attending
  • Additional papers where a party was previously married or lives abroad

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

General answers only. Your case may differ, so please contact us to confirm.

Who can have a court marriage?

Under the Special Marriage Act, 1954, two eligible adults may marry by civil ceremony irrespective of religion. We check eligibility for your case before anything is filed.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the type of marriage and the procedure at the registrar’s office. After reviewing your documents we give you a realistic estimate.

Are witnesses required?

Witnesses are usually required. We tell you how many are needed and which identity documents they should bring.

Is my information kept confidential?

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and to handle your case.
