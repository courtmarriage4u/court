FAQ

Frequently asked questions

General answers only. Your case may differ, so please contact us to confirm.

Is it mandatory to register a Will in India?

Registration is not legally mandatory, as an unregistered Will signed by the testator and attested by two independent witnesses is valid. However, registering a Will at the Sub-Registrar’s office adds strong legal authenticity and significantly reduces the chances of future disputes.

What is the difference between a Probate and a Succession Certificate?

Probate is a court-certified copy of a valid Will confirming the authority of the executor to distribute the deceased person’s assets (both movable and immovable).

Succession Certificate is issued by a civil court when a person passes away without leaving a Will (intestate), authorizing legal heirs to claim movable assets like bank balances, fixed deposits, shares, and insurance proceeds.

When is a Probate mandatory?

Under the Indian Succession Act, Probate of a Will is legally mandatory in specific jurisdictions (such as Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata) for immovable property situated there, and is often required by banks or housing societies elsewhere to transfer high-value assets.

What happens if a person passes away without leaving a Will?

When someone dies without a Will (intestate), their assets are distributed strictly according to the succession laws applicable to their religion, and legal heirs typically need a Succession Certificate or Letters of Administration to claim the assets.
