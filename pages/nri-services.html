FAQ

Frequently asked questions

General answers only. Your case may differ, so please contact us to confirm.

I live abroad. Do I have to travel to India for every court hearing?

Not necessarily. In many stages of matrimonial proceedings—especially mutual consent divorces and interim hearings—courts allow representation through a Special Power of Attorney (SPA) holder (such as a parent or trusted relative) and permit appearances via video conferencing, subject to the court’s approval.

Is a divorce decree granted by a foreign court valid in India?

A foreign divorce decree is valid in India only if it meets strict legal conditions under Section 13 of the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC). For instance, if the marriage took place in India under Hindu law, a contested ex-parte divorce obtained abroad on grounds not recognized in India may be challenged and declared invalid by Indian courts.

What should I do if a Look-Out Circular (LOC) or criminal complaint is filed against me while I am abroad?

You should immediately engage legal counsel in India to check the status of the FIR or LOC, apply for anticipatory bail or exemption from personal appearance, and file appropriate petitions before the High Court to quash or suspend the LOC so you can travel safely without risk of detention at immigration.
