IMAGE FOLDERS - how to swap pictures yourself
Replace a file with a new one that has the SAME NAME and the site updates. No code change needed.

images/logo/
  logo.png          Main logo (header and footer, 512 x 512 px)
  logo-large.png    Large copy of the logo (1024 x 1024 px)
  favicon.png       Small icon in the browser tab (96 x 96 px)

images/backgrounds/   (a dark navy layer is placed over these automatically so text stays readable)
  hero-home.jpg        Home page top banner (wide, at least 1920 x 800 px)
  banner-services.jpg  Marriage Registration page banner (keep faces in the upper part)
  banner-about.jpg     About Us and NRI Services banners, and the picture beside "About Us" on the Home page
  banner-default.jpg   Family & Matrimonial Law, Wills & Probate, Insights, Contact and legal pages

images/blog/
  Article photos uploaded in the Insights console are saved here (about 1600 px wide, under 500 KB each).
  sample-1.jpg to sample-3.jpg are unused examples; you can delete them.

Tip: avoid pictures with watermarks or small logos in the corners.
