HINDI AND DISCLAIMER POP-UP - NOTES
 - Every page has an English | हिंदी switch. Hindi pages live under /hi/. The choice is remembered in the visitor's browser.
 - Hindi wording is in i18n/hi.json. The Hindi text is a DRAFT translation: have it reviewed before launch.
 - Disclaimer pop-up: shown on the first visit (not on the Disclaimer page), in the visitor's language.
   "I Agree" is remembered for 180 days in that browser. "I Disagree" takes the visitor to google.com.
 - The pop-up text is written once in pages/disclaimer.html and copied into the pop-up automatically.
