COURT MARRIAGE FOR U - FINAL SITE: DEPLOYMENT GUIDE
====================================================
This package is the complete website (English and Hindi) with the Insights console.
It must be deployed from GitHub to Netlify (the console and the Insights pages need Netlify's automatic build).

WHAT IS INSIDE
 layout.html            Header, menu and footer shared by every page
 pages/                 The page contents (English). Each file is one page.
 i18n/hi.json           All Hindi wording ("English text": "Hindi text")
 content/insights/      Articles written in the console
 images/                Logo, background pictures and article photos (see images/README.txt)
 admin/                 The Insights console (/admin)
 build.js, netlify.toml The site build (Netlify runs it automatically)
 styles.css, script.js  Design and behaviour
 _headers, _redirects   Security settings and a redirect from the old /services.html

PAGES (each in English and Hindi, Hindi under /hi/)
 Home, About Us, Marriage Registration, Family & Matrimonial Law, NRI Services, Wills & Probate,
 Insights, Contact Us, Disclaimer, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Thank you, and a 404 page.

STEP 1 - PUT THE FILES ON GITHUB
 1. GitHub > "+" > New repository. Name: courtmarriage4u-site. Choose Private. Create.
 2. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip this package and drag EVERYTHING inside the folder
    (all files AND folders) into the page. Click "Commit changes".
    Check the files sit at the top level of the repository (not inside an extra folder).
 3. Open admin/config.yml, click the pencil icon, and change
        repo: YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME/YOUR-REPOSITORY-NAME
    to your real names (example: repo: yourname/courtmarriage4u-site). Commit changes.

STEP 2 - CONNECT NETLIFY
 1. Netlify > Add new site > Import an existing project > GitHub > courtmarriage4u-site.
 2. Do not change the settings (they come from netlify.toml). Click Deploy. Wait 1-2 minutes.
 3. If you earlier deployed by drag-and-drop, remove courtmarriage4u.com from that old site
    (Domain management) before adding it here.

STEP 3 - YOUR DOMAIN (GoDaddy)
 1. In the new Netlify site: Domain management > Add a domain > courtmarriage4u.com.
 2. Netlify shows the DNS records it needs. In GoDaddy > DNS, add exactly those records and remove old
    "parked" records for the main address and www. (If you use GoDaddy email, add the records; do not change nameservers.)
 3. Wait for DNS (minutes to a few hours). Netlify then issues the free HTTPS certificate by itself.

STEP 4 - MAKE FORM ENQUIRIES REACH YOUR EMAIL
 The site has three forms: "callback" (Home), "enquiry" (Contact) and "registration" (Marriage Registration).
 Netlify > your site > Forms. After you submit a test on the live site, the forms appear there.
 Forms > Form notifications > Add notification > Email notification > courtmarriage4u@gmail.com.
 Add one for each of the three forms.
 Your Privacy Policy says submissions are kept for one year: set a yearly reminder to delete older ones in Netlify > Forms.

STEP 5 - THE INSIGHTS CONSOLE (write and edit articles)
 1. GitHub > your picture > Settings > Developer settings > Personal access tokens > Fine-grained tokens > Generate new token.
 2. Name: Insights console. Expiration: 1 year. Repository access: Only select repositories > courtmarriage4u-site.
 3. Repository permissions > Contents: Read and write. Generate and COPY the token. Keep it private.
 4. Open https://courtmarriage4u.com/admin > "Sign In Using Access Token" > paste the token.
 5. Insights (Articles) > New Article. Fill in the title, date, category, language, summary, cover photo (about 1600 px wide,
    under 500 KB) with a short description, and the text. Leave "Draft" off and click Publish.
    The article appears on the Insights page in 1-2 minutes. Turn "Draft" on to hide an article.
 6. Set a reminder to create a new token before it expires in a year.

TEST CHECKLIST (do this once the site is live)
 [ ] Home loads and the first-visit Disclaimer pop-up appears; "I Agree" closes it.
 [ ] The English | हिंदी switch works on every page and remembers the choice.
 [ ] Call and WhatsApp buttons work on your phone.
 [ ] Send a test from each of the three forms and check the email arrives.
 [ ] Open /admin, sign in, and publish a test article, then delete it.
 [ ] Open the site on a phone and check the menu, the sticky Call/WhatsApp bar and the map.

BEFORE YOU ANNOUNCE THE SITE
 - Have a lawyer or translator review the Hindi text (i18n/hi.json), especially the legal wording. It is a draft translation.
 - Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are English-only for now; Hindi pages show a short Hindi note about this.
 - Check the content once more against the Bar Council of India rules, as you planned.
 - Create your Google Business Profile so the map listing matches the address on the site.

EDITING LATER
 - Photos and logo: replace the files in images/ (same file names).
 - English wording: edit the file in pages/ on GitHub. If you change an English sentence, add its Hindi line to i18n/hi.json
   (the Netlify deploy log lists any sentence that has no Hindi version yet).
 - Disclaimer pop-up text: pages/disclaimer.html (the pop-up copies it automatically).

KNOWN LIMITS
 - Photos are not resized automatically; upload them at about 1600 px wide.
 - An article with a future date stays hidden until the site next updates on or after that date.
 - Forms use Netlify's built-in spam filter and a hidden trap field; no CAPTCHA is included.
 - Analytics is not included.
